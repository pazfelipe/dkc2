Donkey Kong Country 2: study port

Game logic ported to TypeScript from the community disassembly. Graphics, music and data are read from your own ROM, which stays in this browser.

Pick your Donkey Kong Country 2 (USA, Rev 1) ROM (.sfc), or drop it here.


      

      
Arrows move · Z jump · A run/roll · X team-up · Enter start · Shift swap · F2/F4 save/load state · ` debug · P pause · H HD Diddy on/off
